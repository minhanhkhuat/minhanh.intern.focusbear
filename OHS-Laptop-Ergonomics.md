# Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) for Desk-Based Work
## Reflection
1. Equipment changes: Using external monitor
2. Behavioural changes: sitting upright, taking regular breaks, enough light.
3. Use Focus Bear to schedule movement breaks.

## Tasks: 
1. Placed my laptop on an elevated stand so the top third of the display sits directly at eye level, removing the need to tilt my head downward.
2. Deployed an external mouse and keyboard at desk level, allowing my shoulders to stay relaxed and elbows to rest naturally at a 90-degree angle.
3. Configured built-in Focus Bear reminders to mandate a 2-minute physical stretching break after every deep focus work session, successfully reducing afternoon stiffness.
