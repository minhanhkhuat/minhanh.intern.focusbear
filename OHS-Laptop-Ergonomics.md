# Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) for Desk-Based Work
## Reflection
1. Equipment changes: Using external monitor
2. Behavioural changes: sitting upright, taking regular breaks, enough light.
3. Use Focus Bear to schedule movement breaks.

## Tasks: 
1. Workspace setup:
- Chair Position: Adjusted the seat height so my feet rest completely flat on the floor, allowing my thighs to be parallel to the ground. The armrests are dialed in so my shoulders remain fully relaxed, and my elbows maintain a precise 90-degree bend level with the desk surface.
- Monitor Position (Laptop Stand): Raised the laptop on a mechanical stand so the top third of the screen sits exactly at my eye level (approximately 50 cm away from my face). This completely eliminates the need to tilt my head downward while writing or reviewing scripts.
- Input Devices: External mouse and keyboard are centered directly in front of me at desk level, preventing any forward reaching or wrist hyperextension.
- Break Timing (Focus Bear Setup): Configured Focus Bear to enforce a strict 2-minute physical stretching break every 45 minutes of deep work, paired with the 20-20-20 eye strain rule.

4. Configured built-in Focus Bear reminders to mandate a 2-minute physical stretching break after every deep focus work session, successfully reducing afternoon stiffness.
