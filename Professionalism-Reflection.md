# Professionalism at Focus Bear
## Reflection
1. Great teamwork and professionalism
- Radical Transparency: Everything is written down with clear, structured context. Tasks include the underlying "why" so no one has to guess the expectations.
- Respect for Deep Work: The team relies heavily on asynchronous communication. They avoid vague, ad-hoc interruptions and respect dedicated focus blocks.
- Objective Feedback: Critiques focus entirely on code logic and data solutions, never on the person. Teammates assume good intent and provide specific, actionable alternatives to help each other grow.
2. I will ensure my text communication is highly accessible by using clean formatting, bullet points, and explicit context. Because team members have varied communication styles and backgrounds, I will avoid vague phrases and state my points clearly so nobody has to guess the underlying intent.
3. I will contribute to a positive culture by actively engaging in team channels, celebrating peer milestones, and maintaining a supportive attitude. Being reliable, meeting my commitments, and showing up ready to collaborate ensures the environment remains low-stress and enjoyable for everyone.
4. I will prepare for check-ins by organizing my technical updates ahead of time. I will list exactly what tasks I have completed, note down my active focus hours tracker data, and outline any specific technical blockers I am facing so we can use the meeting time efficiently.
5. Being proactive means seeking out solutions before asking for help. If I hit a wall with a data transformation script or a SQL query, I will spend time researching documentation, checking logs, and testing dummy datasets first. When I do ask for assistance, I will clearly present what I have already tried.
6. Follow-Up and Escalation Strategy
- Initial Follow-Up: If I need data or an approval from a colleague, I will drop a polite, asynchronous message tagging them in the relevant issue or Slack thread, allowing them a reasonable window to respond during their focus blocks.
- Escalation: If a critical bottleneck remains unanswered for more than 24 to 48 hours and it entirely blocks my task progression, I will bump the thread with a clear reason why the item is urgent. If silence persists, I will gently bring it up to my direct supervisor during the next synchronous standup checkpoint to help unblock the task.
7. What I Won't Do
- I won't disappear: I will never leave questions unanswered or pull requests abandoned without communication.
- I won't mix environments: I will never enter unanonymized company data, internal files, or credentials into public AI models.
- I won't bypass process: I will never merge code or push script changes directly into production without proper automated bot checks, peer review, and authorization.
